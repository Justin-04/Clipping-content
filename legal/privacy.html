Privacy Policy

App: Clipping · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clipping ("the App") is a personal desktop tool that uploads video clips to the TikTok account of the person running it. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok account basics (via Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID and display name, used only to identify the connected account.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload the videos you approve (scopes video.upload / video.publish).
  • Videos, titles and captions that you choose to upload.

2. How data is used

Data is used only to upload your approved videos to your own TikTok account. The App does not read your messages, followers, or other TikTok content, and does not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and video files are stored only on the computer running the App. The App has no server and does not send your data anywhere except to TikTok's official API when uploading.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is sent to TikTok only as needed to upload your content, and TikTok's own privacy policy applies to it there.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the App. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and all local data can be deleted by removing the App's files. You may also contact us to request deletion.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Justin-04/Clipping-content/issues
